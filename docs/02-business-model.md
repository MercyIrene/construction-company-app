# 02 · Business Model & Financial Design

All KES figures nominal 2026; USD at an assumed 130 KES/USD planning rate.

## 1. Revenue architecture

Revenue lines are sequenced to match trust accumulation — each new line is sold *into* relationships the previous line created.

### 1.1 Phase 1 — Delivery (live from month 1)

| Item | Pricing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Project setup fee** | KES 100K–250K fixed, tiered by project size | Covers feasibility review, site assessment, team assembly, contract pack, permit roadmap. Paid upfront; qualifies serious buyers; non-refundable but creditable against delivery fee at contract signature. |
| **Delivery management fee** | 5–8% of certified construction value | 8% < KES 10M, 6.5% KES 10–25M, 5% > KES 25M. Billed per milestone as certified (revenue matches cost cadence). Includes weekly site inspection, evidence pack, disbursement administration, schedule & budget management. |
| **Design coordination fee** (when we assemble the design team) | 10% of design fees | Charged to homeowner transparently. **Never a hidden kickback from professionals** — trust position forbids it (see §4). |

### 1.2 Phase 2 — Network (months 12–24)

- **Match success fee** paid by professional: 5% of their contract value on first project with a client we introduce, tapering to 2.5% for repeat. Charged to the pro, disclosed to the owner.
- **Pro SaaS**: KES 2,500–10,000/mo per firm for pipeline, bid tools, evidence tooling, Reliability Score profile. Free tier during data-accumulation period (adoption > revenue early).
- **Bid management**: standardized tendering on QS-prepared BoQs; fee bundled into delivery fee, listed separately for transparency.

### 1.3 Phase 2–3 — Materials & money

- **Materials procurement**: negotiated supplier rates; we pass through most savings, retain 2–5% margin, and eliminate the single biggest leakage channel (materials fraud). Only after delivery scale gives buying power. Deliveries verified via the same evidence system.
- **Financial services (partner-led, referral/margin income):** construction-linked savings & financing (bank/sacco partners disburse against our verified milestones — this is *the* institutional unlock for construction lending in Kenya), contractor all-risk & workmanship insurance, payment-guarantee products.
- **Escrow economics:** interest on funds in project accounts accrues to the owner by default (trust position). Our income is the disbursement administration already in the delivery fee; any float-sharing arrangement with the partner bank is disclosed.

### 1.4 Phase 3 — Home OS

- **Maintenance plans**: KES 3,000–15,000/mo (inspection cadence, priority dispatch of verified pros, warranty tracking).
- **Renovation projects**: same delivery model, smaller tickets, near-zero CAC (existing Home File customers).
- **Home File**: free forever for delivery customers (it is the moat, not the product); paid onboarding for homes we didn't build (KES 25–50K digitization service).

## 2. Unit economics — Phase 1 reference project

Reference: KES 15M construction value, 14-month build, Kiambu county.

**Revenue:** setup 150K + delivery 6.5% × 15M = 975K → **KES 1,125K (~$8.7K)**

**Direct costs:**

| Cost | KES |
|---|---|
| Delivery manager time (0.1 FTE × 14mo, loaded 350K/mo) | 490K |
| Site inspection visits (fortnightly QA inspector + transport, beyond DM weekly) | 84K |
| Milestone certifications (external QS spot-certification, 6 of 12 milestones) | 90K |
| Payments/SMS/WhatsApp/storage per-project tech cost | 21K |
| **Total direct** | **~685K** |

**Contribution: ~KES 440K (~39%)** at pilot-era load (DM carrying ~8 projects). At target load (12 projects/DM, routed inspections, software-automated evidence review): direct cost ≈ 430K → **contribution ~62%**. The contribution-margin ramp *is* the product roadmap: every Phase-1 feature exists to raise projects-per-DM without quality loss (tracked as the **load ratio** KPI, floor at quality gates — doc 07 §5).

**CAC (blended target):** ≤ KES 120K diaspora (webinars, community partnerships, referral bonuses), ≤ 60K local. Payback within first three milestones. Referral flywheel target: ≥ 40% of projects from referrals by month 18.

Sensitivity: the model breaks if (a) delivery fee < 4.5% sustained, or (b) load ratio stuck < 6 — both are tripwires in the validation plan (doc 03).

## 4. Pricing & trust rules (non-negotiable)

1. **We never take undisclosed money from the supply side.** Every professional-side fee is published. The moment homeowners suspect we're paid to steer them, the company dies.
2. **Interest on client funds belongs to the client** unless explicitly contracted otherwise.
3. **Fees are quoted against the certified BoQ**, not invoiced spend — we must never profit from cost overruns.
4. **No pay-to-play in the Reliability Score.** Score is computable from verifiable events only (doc 05 §4); commercial relationships cannot touch it.

## 5. Why customers pay (value math shown to buyers)

For a KES 15M project, documented failure modes commonly cost: materials leakage 5–15% (0.75–2.25M), rework from unsupervised defects 3–10%, delay carrying costs (rent + inflation) 2–5%, plus tail risk of total loss on abandonment. Msingi's ~7.5% all-in fee prices *below the expected loss it eliminates*, before valuing time, stress, and the Home File. Sales collateral leads with this arithmetic.

## 6. Funding plan

- **Pre-seed/seed: USD 1.5M** for 24 months → milestones: 60+ cumulative projects, load ratio ≥ 8, NPS ≥ 60, referral share ≥ 40%, Phase-1 product complete, Phase-2 network pilot live.
- Use: 45% delivery ops & quality, 30% engineering/product (lean team of 4–6, see doc 12), 15% growth, 10% G&A/legal/compliance.
- **Series A (month ~24): USD 4–6M** on delivery-economics proof, to open the network, materials line, and first bank partnership.
- Structure note: operating company in Kenya (Msingi Build Technologies Ltd); holding-company topco per investor requirements; client funds never on our balance sheet (ADR-0007).

## 7. Key financial risks

| Risk | Exposure | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue recognized on milestones that stall | Cash gap | Setup fee upfront; milestone billing includes a mobilization tranche; stall-rate KPI |
| FX (diaspora pay USD-linked expectations, costs in KES) | Margin noise | Price in KES; accept USD via partner rails at spot |
| Bad-debt on delivery fees | Low (fees deducted at disbursement) | Fee collection is structurally senior: deducted within the milestone payment flow |
| Project abandonment mid-build | Reputation + revenue | Feasibility gate at setup (affordability check); phased contracting; pause-and-preserve protocol (doc 07 §6) |
